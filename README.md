# Bitcoin-calculator-Privacy-Policy-

**Application:** Crypto calculator (also shown as Bitcoin calculator)  
**Last updated:** September 12, 2026  
**Country:** Ukraine  

This Privacy Policy describes how Crypto calculator (“the Application”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Service. It is based on the structure of a standard mobile privacy policy and describes only what this Application actually does.

The Application converts a coin amount to USDT using public market quotes and shows a price chart. It is a calculator, not a broker. It does not create accounts and does not ask you to deposit money.

## Interpretation and Definitions

**Account** — a registered login. This Application does not create Accounts.  
**Application** — Crypto calculator (Bitcoin calculator), the software program provided by us.  
**Company / We / Us** — the developer of Crypto calculator, operating from Ukraine.  
**Country** — Ukraine.  
**Device** — any device that can access the Service, such as a phone or tablet.  
**Personal Data** — any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.  
**Service** — the Application.  
**Service Provider** — a third party that processes data to help provide the Service (for example Google Mobile Ads, CoinMarketCap, or an exchange API).  
**You** — the individual using the Application.

## What stays on the Device

We do not ask you for an email address, name, or password to use the calculator.

The following stays on your Device using local storage (SharedPreferences):

- last selected coin
- last selected exchange
- chart options (timeframe and indicators)

The amount you type is used only for the on-device calculation. It is not saved as a user profile and is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate our own backend for this Application.

## What leaves the Device

To show quotes, charts, and ads, the Application sends technical requests over the internet.

### Coin list

The list of coins is requested from the CoinMarketCap public API.

### Prices and charts

Live USDT prices and candlestick data are requested from the public API of the exchange you select: Binance, Huobi (HTX), OKX, Bybit, or KuCoin. Those companies receive a standard request (for example the coin symbol and your IP address as seen by their servers).

### Advertising

The Application shows a banner ad labeled “Ad”, served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect or use:

- advertising ID
- IP address
- device and ad performance information

This is used to show and measure ads. See Google’s own privacy policy for AdMob.

### Binance referral links

If you tap **Buy on Binance** or **Open**, the Application opens Binance in a browser or the Binance app, including a referral parameter. That is a third-party site. Binance then applies its own privacy policy. We do not execute trades or hold funds.

## Referral / commission disclosure

Binance buttons use a regular referral URL. If you register or trade on Binance after that tap, the developer may receive a commission from Binance. This is not a sale of your calculator inputs.

## How we use information

We use local settings only to remember your last coin, exchange, and chart options.

We do not:

- create or manage an Account
- send you marketing email or SMS
- sell the amount you typed or the coin you selected to data brokers
- use your calculator inputs for promotional campaigns

Third parties listed above use the technical data they receive to provide their APIs or ads.

## Sharing of information

We do not sell Personal Data.

Information may reach third parties only as described above:

- CoinMarketCap — coin list
- the selected exchange — prices and candles
- Google (AdMob) — advertising
- Binance — only if you tap a Binance button

We may disclose information if required by law or a valid request from public authorities.

## Retention

Local settings remain on your Device until you clear the Application data or uninstall it.

We do not keep a copy of your typed amount or selected coin on our servers.

Ad and API providers retain data according to their own policies.

## Transfer

API and ad providers may process requests on servers outside Ukraine or your country. By using the Application you understand that those technical requests leave your Device.

## Delete your data

Because there is no Account, there is nothing to delete on our servers.

You can remove local settings by clearing the Application storage in the Android system settings or by uninstalling the Application.

To ask a question about privacy, email us. We will help as far as we can with data held by us. We cannot delete data held only by Google, CoinMarketCap, an exchange, or Binance; you must use their own tools.

## Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Application in a way that sent Personal Data to a third party, contact us.

## Links to other websites

The Application may open third-party sites or apps, including Binance. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before you register or trade.

## Security

No transmission over the internet is completely secure. We do not store your calculator inputs on our servers. Third-party APIs and ads use HTTPS.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when data sources, ads, or the Application change. The new version is posted on this page and the “Last updated” date is changed. The current text is also available in the Application under **About**.
